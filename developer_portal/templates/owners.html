Link an owner's Facebook account

  1. Make a code here. It works once, for 15 minutes. After 5 wrong tries from one account, or 20 overall in an hour, linking pauses for an hour.
  2. From the owner's own Facebook account, send the code to the store's Page in Messenger, like LINK ABCD-2345.
  3. Reload this page and press Approve next to them. Nothing reaches them, and they can't change orders, until you do.
  4. They get a "You're linked" message, and the other owners get an FYI.

While the Meta app is in Development mode, the Page can only message people who have a role on the app. Add each owner under App roles → Roles in the Meta developer dashboard (Tester is enough) and have them accept the invite.

Facebook lets the Page message someone only within 24 hours of their last message to it, and Meta retired the message tags that used to allow updates after that. So each owner gets a daily check-in with a 👍 button. Tapping it, or replying to any alert, keeps alerts on for another 24 hours. Alerts that can't be delivered wait and arrive the next time that owner messages the Page.
